Round two: what we need from the shop website
From: the shop owner
To: the dev and QA team
Hi team,
Thanks for the first round. Here is the next batch. It is the same shop, so the same rules apply: build against it, test against it, and ask me if something isn't clear. I've written down what I want, not how to check it. That part is up to you.
1. Search
1.1	Customers can search by typing a word and clicking the search button, or by pressing Enter. Both should give the same products.
1.2	If nothing matches, the page should say so and show no products.
1.3	There's a small clear button next to the search box. Clicking it should empty the box and bring back the full list of products.
2. Sorting
2.1	Customers can sort by price from high to low, so the most expensive product comes first, and by name from Z to A.
2.2	Sorting has to work inside a category too. In Power Tools, sorting by price should show only power tools in the right order, and the page should still say which category it is.
3. Cart
3.1	On a product page, customers can type any quantity they like, not only use the plus and minus buttons. The cart should show the quantity they typed.
3.2	In the cart, customers can change the quantity of an item. The line price, the cart total and the number on the cart icon should all follow.
3.3	For eco products the cart total comes out a bit lower than the lines add up to. That's a discount and it's on purpose. Please don't report it as a bug, but do make sure the line price itself is right.
3.4	Customers can remove an item from the cart. When the last one goes, the cart should say it is empty.
4. Contact form
4.1	The Contact page has first name, last name, email, subject (a dropdown) and message. All five are needed before it can be sent.
4.2	If someone presses Send with fields empty, the form should say what's missing, one message under each empty field.
4.3	While someone fills the form in, it should keep what they have typed and chosen.
One warning. Anything sent through this form reaches my real inbox. Please fill it in and check it all you like, but never press Send on a form that is filled in.
5. Things I haven't decided
5.1	Some products are out of stock. I haven't decided what message to show, but nobody should be able to add one to the cart.
6. Not now
Logging in, checkout and payment, and order history. Those come later, so leave them out of this round.
Thanks,
The shop owner
